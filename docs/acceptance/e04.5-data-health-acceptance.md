## Delivered capability

E04.5 adds a deterministic, rebuildable operational truth layer:

```text
Persisted observations, cursors and runs
  -> Topic × Source coverage projection
  -> collector health, freshness and data-quality aggregation
  -> read-only Operations/Coverage APIs and verification CLI
  -> /operations and compact Topic coverage summaries
```

Migration `20260811_0005_data_health_coverage.py` creates
`topic_source_coverage`. The projection stores target and observed windows, actual/missing counts,
partial reasons, source strategy and `derivation_version = coverage-v1`. It does not modify Raw
evidence or collector observation semantics.

## Known limitations and deliberately deferred work

- E04 cross-day acceptance is complete. Five real snapshot dates exist through `2026-08-19`, while
  the missing dates `2026-08-12`, `2026-08-15`, `2026-08-16` and `2026-08-17` remain explicitly
  missing rather than interpolated.
- E03 remains in its real seven-day operational soak; manual runs do not count as scheduler days.
- Community/Hacker News and Public/Wikipedia collectors are not implemented.
- No Trend Engine, Trend Score, momentum, acceleration, forecast, cross-source correlation, AI
  diagnosis, alerts, Prometheus/Grafana stack, Airflow, Kafka or Kubernetes was added.
- Raw integrity results are intentionally not persisted yet; API/page state remains not verified
  between explicit CLI checks rather than displaying a false zero.
- Backup/restore, manifest verification and isolated restore drills are implemented under E04.5B.
  Operational backup refresh and an off-host copy remain ongoing responsibilities.
